# Data processing

You may want to enlarge a small movement, wait for two participants, move a signal across many destinations, or generate variation without a performer. These examples show the results in plots and value displays; connect them to sound, light or images afterwards.

## Choose a response

- **Make a signal fit its destination:** start with [Math expressions and structured objects]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/data/maths-and-objects.html). Change the scale and offset of a signal, then extract single fields from a larger message.
- **Control when a response happens:** try [Mapping and event utilities]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/data/mapping-overview.html). Filter repeated input, retain a short history, or wait for fresh events from two sources.
- **Treat many destinations as an ensemble:** open [Address pattern tools]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/data/address-tools.html). Distribute a list of values or move a signal through a group instead of wiring every destination individually.
- **Let a signal evolve by itself:** use [Delayed value feedback]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/data/value-feedback.html), where one coefficient decides whether a recurrence settles or keeps varying.

For a short first exercise, open the feedback example and change its coefficient from 3.68 to 2.5. Watch the trace settle, then restore the original value to bring back the irregular motion. The example displays the control signal and produces no sound.
