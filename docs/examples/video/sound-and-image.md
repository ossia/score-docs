# Timed sound and image effects

![Video distortion controls above timed audiovisual automation curves]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/scores/thumbnails/examples-video-sound-and-image.png)

Arrange image degradation and changes in acoustic space as parts of the same timed piece. A one-minute movie develops compression artifacts and analog-style distortion while an independent audio arrangement moves through recorded voices, a pause and a looping amen break.

## Compose together, edit separately

Automation curves give the image effects a repeatable trajectory, while a drifting modulation adds variation. The audio has its own edits and automated reverberation. Keeping the sources in separate processes lets you change their relationship: a visual change can coincide with a sound edit, continue through silence or build at another pace.

## Try it

Open the ZIP directly in score. Use a build with JPeg and Aether, configure an audio output, and start at a low listening level.

- Play from the beginning and compare the movie's roughly 60-second duration with the audio edits. Listen for the pause while watching whether the image continues.
- Change one video automation curve to move or reshape a distortion gesture. Compare its timing with the existing sound arrangement.
- Change the reverberation automation without changing the image. Notice how the same picture can acquire a different sense of space.

Replacing the movie does not replace the audio: reconnect or edit the sound sources separately when adapting the piece.
