# Timed sound and image effects

![Video distortion controls above timed audiovisual automation curves]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/scores/thumbnails/examples-video-sound-and-image.png)

Arrange image degradation and changes in acoustic space as parts of the same timed piece. A one-minute movie develops compression artifacts and analog-style distortion while an independent audio arrangement moves through recorded voices, a pause and a looping amen break.

## Separate image and sound processes

Automation curves give the image effects a repeatable trajectory, while a drifting modulation adds variation. The audio has its own edits and automated reverberation. Because the sources are separate processes, a visual change can coincide with a sound edit, continue through silence or build at another pace.

## Try it

Open the ZIP directly in score. Use a build with JPeg and Aether, configure an audio output, and start at a low listening level.

- Play from the beginning. The movie runs for roughly 60 seconds independently of the audio edits; watch the image during the pause in the sound.
- Change one video automation curve to move or reshape a distortion so it lands on a different sound edit.
- Change the reverberation automation without changing the image: the same picture now sits in a different acoustic space.

Replacing the movie does not replace the audio: reconnect or edit the sound sources separately when adapting the piece.
