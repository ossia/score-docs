# Basic Examples

## Timeline and interaction

- [Basic timeline]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/timeflo.html): progress from a linear sample arrangement to triggers and loops.
- [Timeline and patches]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/timeline-and-patches.html): activate sources and effects independently.
- [Time model]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/timemodel.html): compare fixed, flexible and bounded intervals.
- [Temporal patterns]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/timemodel-patterns.html): explore retriggering, event conditions and joins.
- [Cues, loops and exclusive playback]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/cues.html): recall parameters and switch audio clips.
- [Process presets, cues and automations]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/process-cues-and-automations.html): control native volume shaders over time.

## Dataflow and observation

- [Multi-media patching]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/all-media.html): connect MIDI, audio analysis, values and native 3D rendering.
- [Combining lists and selecting routes]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/combine-spread-mux-demux.html): compare Combine/Spread and Mux/Demux.
- [Weighted and time-based interpolation]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/interpolation.html): blend control signals and ease transitions.
- [Monitoring]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/monitoring.html): inspect values, MIDI, audio levels and textures.
- [Basic OSC]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/osc.html): send cues, automate addresses and receive triggers.
- [Recording sensor messages and automations]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/recording.html): compare curve and message recording.
- [Temporal patching: glutton]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/glutton.html): process the currently active audio source.
- [Temporal patching: delayed]({{ site.baseurl }}/examples/basics/delayed.html): deliver earlier source audio to later effect intervals.
